# Plan: Bank of Bloody Mary

User: the blood-bank operations manager. Question: which fridge to service or evacuate this shift, before red cells leave 1–6 °C. Target 3.5 °C is the middle of the spec. It is not the control-chart center.

## Formulas
A subgroup is the 4 readings in one hour. Variance divides by n − 1. σ_short = √(mean of the baseline subgroup variances). σ_overall = sample sd of every baseline reading. X-bar center = grand mean. Limits and zones = center ± k σ_short/√n for k = 1, 2, 3. d2 and d3 are the mean and sd of the range of n standard normals, from `ptukey` with infinite degrees of freedom. Range center = d2 σ_short. Range upper = (d2 + 3 d3) σ_short. Range lower = max(0, (d2 − 3 d3) σ_short). Cpk = min(6 − mean, mean − 1) / (3 σ_short). Ppk uses σ_overall in that denominator. Gap = Cpk − Ppk. Baseline = the first 48 hours and at least 25 subgroups. Later hours use those frozen limits. Lag-1 is the correlation of each baseline reading with the reading 15 minutes later. Forecast: a straight line on subgroup means after hour 48, then hours until the line hits 1 °C or 6 °C. Rolling Ppk uses the trailing 24 hours. RPN = Severity × Occurrence × Urgency, each scored 1–10. Generator transfer times use an individuals and moving-range chart. There σ_short = (mean moving range) / d2, with d2 computed at n = 2. Upper spec = 10 seconds. Tests `special_cause_test_1` through `_8` run on X-bar. Test 1 also runs on the range chart. A point exactly on a line is not a flag.

## Decisions
Team Keep It Cool. Virtual DL, due Fri Oct 23, 3 PM ET. Prompt: Blood Banks. Repo: github.com/rubberduckysmiles/Rwedoneyet. Stack: Shiny from demos/shinyapp.
Capable: Cpk ≥ 1.0 and no flag means "capable and stable". Cpk ≥ 1.0 with a flag means "inside the spec width, but not stable". Cpk < 1.0 means "not capable".
Units at risk = bags in fridges outside 1–6 °C now, or with any flag in the last 24 h. Needing action = red or amber. Red: a reading outside 1–6 °C in the last 24 h, or two X-bar test-1 points in a row inside that 24 h. Amber: two flags in the last 24 h, Cpk < 1.0, or a crossing within 12 h. Green: otherwise.
The badge threshold is a decision, made after measurement, not a formula. One post-baseline flag was enough at first; that put 92% of 200 simulated healthy fridges at red or amber. Taking the same 24-hour window the bag count uses and asking for two flags in it puts 15.5% there, while a fridge that really drifts out of range is still red 100% of the time (demo/alert_rate.R, data/README.md).
The card sentence on the Overview (`flag_sentence`) reports the newest flag inside that same 24-hour window, so the sentence cannot describe an event the badge was not allowed to count; a clean window reads "No special-cause flag." The capability sentence keeps its whole-chart meaning (`any_flag_post`), so a green fridge with older flags still reads "inside the spec width, but not stable": two sentences, two scopes.
Forecast uses post-baseline hours only. Severity by bags: 1 if 0; then 2 through 10 for 1–5, 6–15, 16–30, 31–50, 51–75, 76–100, 101–150, 151–200, 201+. Occurrence, worst signal: 1 none; 2 test 6; 3 test 5 or 8; 4 test 4; 5 test 2; 6 test 7; 7 test 3; 8 range test 1; 9 X-bar test 1 while inside spec; 10 any reading outside 1–6 °C. Urgency: 10 if already out or within 2 h; then 9, 8, 7, 6, 5, 4, 3, 2, 1 for within 4, 8, 12, 18, 24, 36, 48, 72 h, and more than 72 h or no crossing. Minutes out of spec are shown and are not in the RPN. No dollars. qcc is a cross-check only.

## Risks
1. A packaged chart will not match, because sigma short is the pooled variance, not a range constant. The SPEC numbers are the answer key.
2. The eight tests each run on about 120 post-baseline hours, so a healthy fridge nearly always trips something somewhere: with the first badge rule 92% of 200 simulated healthy fridges came back red or amber. Measured lag-1 autocorrelation is under 0.16 on these readings (about 0.02 on F01 of the demo file), so dependence is not what drove it, and the fix went into the aggregation instead: two flags in the last 24 h, not one. The app still prints lag-1 and still says the limits assume independence.
3. The 24-hour badge window ages out a fault that resolved one to two days ago. The demo's F04 is exactly that case, a blackout on Oct 6, 14:00–16:00, and the shipped rule calls it green where the first rule said red. units_at_risk uses the same window, so the badge and the bag count agree; a two-day window would keep F04 on the list but double the healthy-fleet false alarms, 38.5% instead of 15.5%. Recorded rather than hidden.
4. The card sentence and the badge were computed over different periods: the badge over the last 24 h, the sentence over every flag since the baseline. Nothing failed while that was true, because `flag_sentence` had no test and no README paragraph, so no document and no assertion noticed the two disagreeing. In the demo it made green F01 and F04 announce a shift from two to three days earlier, and gave amber F05 the same sentence as green F01, so a manager could not tell why F05 needed watching. H-32 gave the sentence the badge's window and added `tests/test-flag_sentence.R`, which pins those three cards. The lesson recorded here: an output nobody tests is where a contradiction survives.
4. Judges need a public URL. Deploy as soon as testme runs. The publisher key goes in GitHub secrets, never in a file or in chat.
